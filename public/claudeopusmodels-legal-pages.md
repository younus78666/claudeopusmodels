## Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** May 9, 2026
**Last Updated:** May 9, 2026

### 1. Introduction

Claude Model Insights ("we," "us," "our") operates claudeopusmodels.com. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that data.

This policy applies to all visitors of claudeopusmodels.com, regardless of location. We comply with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

### 2. Information We Collect

#### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

We collect information you voluntarily give us through:

**Contact form submissions:** Your name, email address, and the contents of your message when you reach out through our contact page.

**Newsletter signup:** Your email address if you subscribe to our email updates. We don't collect any other personal data during signup.

We don't require account creation to read our content. We don't process payments or collect billing information.

#### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our site, certain data is collected automatically through cookies and similar technologies:

**Usage data:** Pages you visit, time spent on each page, referring website, date and time of visit, and general browsing patterns.

**Device data:** Browser type and version, operating system, screen resolution, and device type (desktop, mobile, tablet).

**Network data:** IP address, approximate geographic location (city/country level, not street address), and internet service provider.

This data is collected through Google Analytics 4 (GA4) and, if enabled, Microsoft Clarity for heatmap and session replay analysis.

#### 2.3 Information We Don't Collect

We don't collect social security numbers, financial information, health data, biometric data, or data from children under 16. We don't use facial recognition, voice recording, or keystroke logging technologies.

### 3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for these specific purposes:

**Responding to inquiries.** When you contact us, we use your name and email to reply to your message.

**Sending newsletters.** If you subscribe, we send periodic email updates about new articles. Every email includes an unsubscribe link.

**Understanding our audience.** Analytics data helps us understand which articles are most useful, where our readers are located, and how we can improve the site.

**Maintaining site security.** We use technical data to identify and prevent security threats, spam, and abuse.

**Complying with legal obligations.** We may process data when required by applicable laws or regulations.

We don't use your data for automated decision-making, profiling, targeted advertising, or selling to third parties.

### 4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For visitors in the European Union and United Kingdom, we process personal data under these legal bases:

**Consent:** For newsletter subscriptions and non-essential cookies. You can withdraw consent at any time.

**Legitimate interest:** For analytics (understanding our audience), site security, and improving our content. We've assessed that these interests don't override your privacy rights given the limited and non-sensitive nature of the data.

**Legal obligation:** When we're required to retain or disclose data by applicable law.

### 5. How We Share Your Information

**We don't sell your personal information.** We never have, and we don't plan to.

We share data with these categories of service providers, who process it on our behalf:

**Analytics providers:** Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC, USA). Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

**Heatmap and session replay:** Microsoft Clarity (Microsoft Corporation, USA), if enabled. Microsoft's privacy policy: https://privacy.microsoft.com

**Email service provider:** For newsletter delivery, we use an email service that processes subscriber email addresses. The specific provider is identified in our newsletter emails.

**Hosting provider:** Our website hosting provider processes server logs that contain IP addresses and request data.

All service providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and use it only for the purposes we specify.

We may also disclose information when required by law, court order, or government request, or when necessary to protect our rights, safety, or property.

We use cookies on this site. For detailed information about which cookies we use, their duration, and how to manage them, please see our [Cookie Policy](/cookie-policy/).

## Cookie Policy

**Last Updated:** May 9, 2026

### What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, phone, or tablet) when you visit a website. They help websites remember information about your visit, making your next visit easier and the site more useful.

### Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies for two purposes: keeping the site functional and understanding how visitors use it. We don't use cookies for advertising, tracking across websites, or building marketing profiles.

### Types of Cookies We Use

#### Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the site to work properly. They handle basic functions like page navigation and access to secure areas. The site can't function without them.

| Cookie | Provider | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| ghost-members-ssr | claudeopusmodels.com | Session management | Session |
| ghost-members-ssr.sig | claudeopusmodels.com | Session signature | Session |

You can't opt out of essential cookies because they're required for the site to work.

#### Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our site. All data is aggregated and anonymous. We use this information to improve our content and user experience.

| Cookie | Provider | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| _ga | Google Analytics | Distinguishes unique users | 2 years |
| _ga_[ID] | Google Analytics | Maintains session state | 2 years |
| _gid | Google Analytics | Distinguishes unique users | 24 hours |
| _gat | Google Analytics | Throttles request rate | 1 minute |

**Google Analytics 4 (GA4):** We use GA4 with IP anonymization enabled. Google processes this data under their terms of service. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

**Microsoft Clarity** (if enabled): Session replay and heatmap analytics. Data is anonymized. Microsoft's privacy policy: https://privacy.microsoft.com

Analytics cookies are set only with your consent (for EU/UK visitors via our cookie banner).

### Cookies We Don't Use

We don't use:

- Advertising or marketing cookies
- Social media tracking pixels (Facebook Pixel, TikTok Pixel)
- Cross-site tracking cookies
- Fingerprinting or supercookies
- Third-party retargeting cookies

### Managing Your Cookie Preferences

#### Cookie Banner

When you first visit our site (EU/UK visitors), a cookie banner asks for your consent to analytics cookies. You can change your preference at any time by clicking "Cookie Preferences" in the site footer.

#### Browser Settings

You can also control cookies through your browser:

**Chrome:** Settings, then Privacy and Security, then Cookies and Other Site Data

**Safari:** Preferences, then Privacy, then Manage Website Data

**Firefox:** Settings, then Privacy and Security, then Cookies and Site Data

**Edge:** Settings, then Privacy, Search, and Services, then Cookies

**Note:** Blocking all cookies may affect site functionality.

#### Opt-Out Links

**Google Analytics:** Install the opt-out browser add-on at https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout

### Do Not Track

Some browsers send a "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal. There's no industry standard for how websites should respond to DNT. We currently don't alter our data collection practices based on DNT signals, but we respect browser-level cookie blocking.

### Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy when we add or remove cookies. The "Last Updated" date at the top reflects the most recent change.

For questions about our use of cookies:
